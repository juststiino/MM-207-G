Terms of service

Use of the service

Everyone can use this app to find public recipes, but it requires an account and active consent to data processing as described in the privacy policy to create and manage recipes.

User content

Users may create both private and public recipes

  • Private recipes are only visable to the users
  • Public recipes are visible to everyone

Public recipes

Public recipes are considered public contributions. When an account is deleted, public recipes remain available but are anonymized. Users who wish to remove public recipes must delete them before deleting their account.

Consent and deleting account

Consent is requierd to create and manage recipes. If a user withdraw their consent, this is done b deleting the account. Deleting an account removes all personal data and private recipes.
